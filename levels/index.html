How it works

Four steps, start to finish. No charts to watch, no app to open — the levels come to the subscriber.

  1. 1

    The zones are drawn

    From 4H candles: SUPPLY zones (red) sit above price, anchored on swing highs; DEMAND zones (green) sit below, anchored on swing lows. The most tested zone is crowned the 👑 KEY LEVEL.

  2. 2

    A watcher checks the price

    Live price is checked about every 5 minutes while the market is open — the same engine behind the Levels Alarm app.

  3. 3

    Alerts fire on entry only

    An alert fires only when price crosses from outside INTO a zone — starting inside fires nothing. After an alert, that zone stays quiet for 30 minutes, and price has to leave before it can alert again. No spam, no repeats.

  4. 4

    Subscribers get the emails

    The morning Key Levels email — with THE PLAY for each market, the little prediction that comes with the levels — plus a zone-entry alert the moment one fires. Subscribers cancel anytime from their own PayPal.
